Understanding your university offer

Know what the offer asks you to do next.

01

Read the exact conditions

A conditional offer lists requirements you still need to meet. These may concern results, English evidence, documents or other programme requirements. An unconditional offer should still be read carefully for enrolment and payment instructions.

02

Check the details

Confirm your name, programme, campus, qualification, start date, duration and fee information. Ask the institution to correct errors before you proceed.

03

Understand payments

Read deposit instructions, payment deadlines and refund conditions. Verify payment instructions directly with the institution through its official channels.

04

Track deadlines

Make a note of the acceptance date and every outstanding condition. Keep written confirmation of submissions and communications.

05

Plan the next stage

An offer does not itself grant permission to travel or study in a country. Follow the relevant institution and immigration processes for your situation.
