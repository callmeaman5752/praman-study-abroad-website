Study Abroad FAQs

Common questions from students in Nepal

Quick answers before you book a counselling session.

Which country is best for studying abroad from Nepal?

The best destination depends on your academic profile, budget, course, intake, English-language evidence and long-term goals. A profile-based shortlist is more useful than choosing a country only by popularity.

When should I start my study abroad application?

Starting early gives you more time for university research, documents, English testing where needed, financial planning and visa preparation. Exact timelines vary by institution and destination.

Can Praman help with SOP, CV and application documents?

Yes. Praman provides structured application and documentation guidance, including SOP and CV support, while keeping the information based on your genuine academic and personal background.

Do you provide student visa guidance?

Praman supports visa-file preparation and document organisation. Visa decisions are made only by the relevant immigration authority and requirements can change.

Not sure which country is right for you?

Bring your academic profile. We’ll help you understand your options.
