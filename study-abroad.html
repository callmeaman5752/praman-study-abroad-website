How it works

From profile to departure, step by step.

  • Profile and academic assessment
  • Country, course and university shortlist
  • Application and document preparation
  • Offer, finance and visa-file preparation
  • Pre-departure and arrival guidance

What documents should you prepare?

The exact list changes by country, university, programme and applicant. Most applications start with the following:

  • Passport
  • Academic certificates and transcripts
  • English-language test result where required
  • CV / motivation or personal statement
  • Financial documents for visa stage
  • Country-specific supporting documents
Check my documents
Important: immigration and admission rules can change by intake and applicant. The guides are designed for planning and should be checked against the latest official requirement before submission.
