ASK PRAMAN

Study abroad FAQs

Which destination is right for me?

That depends on your academic background, course interests, budget, language evidence and preferred intake. We can help you build a shortlist around those priorities.

What should I bring to counselling?

Bring academic records, your passport if available, English test results if you have them, and a rough budget. Write down your questions and preferred destinations.

Is counselling free?

The initial counselling service is offered as free in Praman’s current service information. Ask the team about any later application or support charges before proceeding.

Can I apply without final results?

Some institutions consider applications while results are pending, but this depends on the programme. Check the institution’s requirements and any offer conditions.

Do I need IELTS or another English test?

Accepted evidence varies by course and institution. Some accept different tests or specific alternatives. Get written confirmation before relying on a waiver.

Can you help with scholarships?

We can help you understand eligibility and the application process. Scholarship decisions and amounts are set by the awarding institution.

Do you guarantee admission or a visa?

No. Institutions decide admissions and scholarships; immigration authorities decide visa applications. Our role is to support informed choices and accurate preparation.

How will I hear back after an enquiry?

Our team will use the phone number or email you provide. If your plans are urgent, please call +977 9715500456.

Can my family join me?

Family eligibility depends on the destination, your programme and current immigration rules. Check the official authority’s guidance for your circumstances.

Should I send my passport through this form?

No. Use the enquiry form for contact details and a brief study plan. Ask your counsellor for an appropriate secure method if documents are needed.

How do I confirm service charges and refunds?

Ask for the service scope and any charges in writing before paying. Institution, testing and immigration fees follow the relevant provider’s policies.

Can I meet your team in person?

Yes. Contact us to arrange a visit at New Plaza, Putalisadak, Kathmandu. Please confirm availability before travelling.
