Privacy Policy

How we handle information submitted through this website.

Information you share with us

When you enquire, we ask for your name, phone number, email address and study interests so our team can respond. Please do not include passwords, passport scans, payment details or financial records in the public enquiry form.

How enquiries are handled

Our forms send your enquiry to pramanstudy@gmail.com through FormSubmit and also save a copy to our Google Sheet through Google Apps Script. We include the page where you made the enquiry. We use this information to answer your questions, arrange counselling and follow up on your study plans. Sending an enquiry does not subscribe you to a newsletter.

Browser storage and third-party services

No advertising pixels or third-party analytics service are installed. A short-lived session marker lets the Thank You page recognise a confirmed form response; it contains no contact details. Google Maps may contact Google when its embedded map loads. FormSubmit, Google Maps, WhatsApp and social platforms follow their own privacy policies.

Your information and choices

Enquiries are handled through our mailbox and FormSubmit. Contact us to ask about information you submitted, request a correction or deletion, or stop further enquiry follow-up. Applicable record-keeping requirements may affect a request. Our team can explain the handling of your particular enquiry.

Contact us

For privacy questions, email info@pramanstudyabroad.edu.np or call 01-4500456 .
