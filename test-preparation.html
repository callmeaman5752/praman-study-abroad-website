Make practice count

Build a routine around your needs.

Start with your current level. Practise consistently, review your mistakes and make time to check your progress.

  1. 01

    Check your starting point

    Complete a current practice test. Record the tasks that take too long and the mistakes that appear most often.

  2. 02

    Practise with a purpose

    Choose one skill to improve each day. Review your work, get feedback where available and repeat the difficult tasks.

  3. 03

    Review your progress

    Complete timed practice regularly. Compare it with your starting point and adjust the next week’s priorities.

Common questions

Before you choose a test

Ask your institution about its current requirements before paying a test fee.

Which English test should I take?

Choose from the tests accepted by your course and application route. Compare the task format, test delivery, available dates and your own strengths.

How long should I prepare?

Your starting level, target score and study time all matter. Use a current practice test to identify what needs work before deciding on a booking date.

Can Praman guarantee my score?

No preparation plan can guarantee a test result. Regular practice and useful feedback can help you improve; your official result is decided by the test provider.
